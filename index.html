PROJECT / 04

A Compact Uni-Spine Slot-Loaded Patch Antenna for C-Band Wireless and UAV Communication Systems

Designed and simulated a compact uni-spine slot-loaded microstrip patch antenna on RT5880 substrate optimised for 5.73 GHz. Achieved return loss (S11) of –35.72 dB, VSWR of 0.976, gain of 6.84 dB, directivity of 6.69 dB, and radiation efficiency ≈ 90%. The compact planar design enables easy PCB fabrication while maintaining stable directional radiation and efficient power transfer. The proposed antenna is suitable for 6 GHz Wi‑Fi, UAV communication, IoT gateways, and other high-speed wireless communication systems.
