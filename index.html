// 01 — ABOUT

Designing the
Future of Wireless

I am a graduate RF & Antenna Engineer from Sai Vidya Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, with specialization in advanced electromagnetic design, 5G systems, and radar applications. My professional journey bridges cutting-edge simulation and real-world RF systems.

My simulation work spans HFSS-designed patch antennas achieving S11 of –32 dB, through to 4×4 phased arrays with active beam-steering at 3.5 GHz — all targeting real-world 5G deployment. I have published two IEEE papers on antenna design optimization.

At DRDO LRDE, I work on defence-grade RF and radar systems. I also serve as IEEE Secretary and Chair of four society student branches at SVIT, driving technical growth and community engagement.

// 03 — PROJECTS

Engineering
Work

PROJECT / 01

High-Performance Sub-6 GHz Patch Antenna with Crescent DGS for 5G

HFSS-designed microstrip patch antenna at 3.5 GHz for 5G NR Band n78. A crescent-shaped defected ground structure dramatically boosted return loss and gain beyond a planar reference.

S11: –32 dBGain: 4.06 dB 3.5 GHzHFSS

● PUBLISHED — IEEE Xplore / ICMNWC 2025

PROJECT / 02

4×4 Phased Array with Beam Steering for Sub-6 GHz 5G

Designed and simulated a 16-element phased array on FR4 substrate with full beam-steering capability. Demonstrates high gain and efficient radiation for 5G base stations.

S11: –22.5 dBGain: 6.87 dB 4×4 ArrayBeam Steering

● ACCEPTED — WAMS 2026 Oral Presentation

PROJECT / 03

RaNaT: IoT-Based Dual-Axis Solar Tracking System

Engineered a real-time IoT solar tracking system focused on sustainability and cost-effectiveness. Embedded sensors and a monitoring dashboard achieved a 15% improvement in energy capture.

+15% EfficiencyReal-time IoT ArduinoEmbedded C

● WINNER — INVENTRONICS Hackathon, SVIT

PROJECT / 04

A Compact Uni-Spine Slot-Loaded Patch Antenna for C-Band Wireless and UAV Communication Systems

Designed and simulated a compact uni-spine slot-loaded microstrip patch antenna on RT5880 substrate optimised for 5.73 GHz. Achieved return loss (S11) of –35.72 dB with exceptional gain performance.
